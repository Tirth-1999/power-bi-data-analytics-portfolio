# Data Professional Survey Breakdown

An interactive Power BI dashboard summarizing the careers, tools, demographics, and workplace satisfaction of 630 data professionals.

![Data Professional Survey dashboard](assets/dashboard-preview.png)

## Business questions

- How does average salary vary by data role?
- Which programming languages are most popular?
- Where are survey respondents located?
- What are the demographic patterns in the sample?
- How satisfied are respondents with their roles and salaries?

## Dashboard highlights

- **630** survey respondents
- **29.87** average respondent age
- **$53.36K** average reported salary
- Salary comparison across data-focused job titles
- Programming-language preference analysis
- Country and gender distribution views
- Role and salary satisfaction gauges

## Power BI skills demonstrated

- KPI card design
- Categorical comparison and ranking
- Demographic segmentation
- Interactive report composition
- Treemap, bar, column, pie, and gauge visuals
- Consistent visual hierarchy and dashboard layout
